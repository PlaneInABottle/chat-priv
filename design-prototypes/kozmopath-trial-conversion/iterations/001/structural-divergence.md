# Structural divergence

## Variant A · Direct Trial

The trial offer is a conventional repeated action layer. It appears in the announcement, header, hero action group, and final conversion section. The approved product conversation remains the dominant proof object; no extra trial card or floating badge competes with it.

## Variant B · Guided

The hero copy has no large primary button. Conversion moves into a product-specific trial runway physically joined to the conversation proof: offer facts lead into account, ikas, and customer-question steps before the local account action. The final section repeats this connected sequence instead of duplicating Variant A's action layout.

## Template-removal test

Removing the actual Kozmopath proof, ikas connection, channel boundaries, handoff dossier, and catalog-signal language leaves neither direction coherent. Variant B's runway also depends on that exact account-to-ikas-to-question sequence; it cannot be relabeled as a generic SaaS trial card without rebuilding the composition.
